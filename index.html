Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know

How do I join Alpha Esports?+
We hold open trials every quarter. Follow us on social media for announcements. You'll need to be in the top tier of the ranked leaderboard and pass our bootcamp evaluation.
Does Alpha provide gaming gear?+
Yes, all our pro players receive sponsor-provided peripherals, custom jerseys, and access to our state-of-the-art gaming facility equipped with high-end PCs and streaming setups.
Can brands partner with Alpha?+
Absolutely. We offer sponsorship packages across multiple tiers. Reach out to our partnerships team at partners@alphaesports.gg for custom proposals.
What games does Alpha compete in?+
We currently field rosters in BGMI, Valorant, and Call of Duty Mobile. We're scouting for fighting games and CS2 for the upcoming season.
How can I watch Alpha matches?+
All our matches are streamed live on our YouTube and Twitch channels. Match schedules are posted on our Discord server and social media handles.
